How to Use This Course

From the course overview

Daily Loop

One lesson, one drill, one submission. Read the technique, watch the demonstration, shoot your take, and check it honestly against the pass checklist before moving on. A pass is a real pass, not close enough.

Weekly Redo

Once a week, reshoot the lesson you passed with the least confidence, the one that felt shakiest even though it technically passed. Skills decay if they are not used again.

Interleave, Don’t Binge

Later lessons deliberately reuse earlier skills in new combinations. If an earlier skill resurfaces and it does not hold up, that is the signal to redo it, not skip past it.

The Capstone Is the Test

Lesson 14 is not a new skill, it is every earlier skill running at once under real conditions. Reshoot the specific scene that reveals a gap, do not reshoot the whole film to avoid it.
